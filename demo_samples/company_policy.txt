ACME Corporation - Employee Handbook 2024

=== Remote Work Policy ===

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

Eligibility:
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation period are eligible for remote work arrangements. Part-time employees may apply on a case-by-case basis with manager approval.

Work Schedule:
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week
- Core hours (10 AM - 3 PM EST) must be maintained for team collaboration
- Employees must be available for video meetings during business hours
- Minimum 2 days per week in office required for team collaboration

Equipment and Technology:
- Company will provide: laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset
- Employee responsible for: stable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps)
- IT support available via Slack or phone during business hours
- VPN access required for all company systems

=== Time Off Policy ===

Vacation Days:
- 0-2 years of service: 15 days per year
- 3-5 years of service: 20 days per year
- 6+ years of service: 25 days per year
- Vacation days accrue monthly and roll over up to 40 days maximum

Sick Leave:
- 10 days per year, unlimited rollover
- Medical documentation required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days
- Can be used for personal illness or caring for immediate family members

Parental Leave:
- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid leave
- Secondary caregiver: 8 weeks paid leave
- Must be used within 12 months of birth or adoption
- Job protection guaranteed upon return

=== Professional Development ===

Learning Budget:
Each employee receives $2,000 annually for professional development, including:
- Online courses and certifications
- Conference attendance
- Books and learning materials
- Professional association memberships

Conference Policy:
- Up to 2 conferences per year with manager approval
- Company covers: registration, travel, accommodation, meals
- Employee must present learnings to team upon return

Tuition Reimbursement:
- Up to $10,000 per year for degree programs
- Must be related to current role or career progression at ACME
- Minimum 2-year commitment after completing program
- Grade requirement: B or higher

=== Health and Wellness ===

Health Insurance:
- Medical, dental, and vision coverage
- Company pays 80% of premiums for employees
- Family coverage available at employee cost-sharing (50/50)
- Coverage begins on first day of employment

Wellness Benefits:
- Gym membership reimbursement: up to $50/month
- Mental health support: 6 free counseling sessions per year through EAP
- Annual health screening: free on-site or reimbursed
- Standing desk stipend: $300 one-time

=== Performance and Compensation ===

Performance Reviews:
- Annual reviews conducted in December
- Mid-year check-ins in June
- 360-degree feedback from peers, managers, and direct reports
- Performance ratings: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement

Salary Adjustments:
- Merit increases based on performance review (typically 3-7%)
- Cost of living adjustments reviewed annually
- Promotions may include 10-15% salary increase
- Salary bands reviewed quarterly against market data

Bonus Structure:
- Quarterly bonuses based on company and individual performance
- Target bonus: 10% of base salary for individual contributors
- Target bonus: 20% of base salary for managers
- Target bonus: 30% of base salary for directors and above

For questions about these policies, contact HR at hr@acmecorp.com or extension 5000.
